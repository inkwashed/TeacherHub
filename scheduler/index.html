Getting started

Set up the Scheduler once, then create correctly timed Google Calendar events in just a few clicks. Your information stays in this browser unless you export it.

  1. 1

    Add your schools and classes

    Rename or add schools, enter their locations and classes, and optionally save each class's main teacher and student count.

  2. 2

    Set your bell times

    Adjust regular periods, optional irregular periods, and lunch settings for each school.

  3. 3

    Save your teaching dates

    Save working hours and teaching dates for each school so the Target Date controls can quickly move between scheduled school days.

  4. 4

    Back up or move your data

    Use Backup & restore in the Teacher Tools header to export or restore one backup covering all of your saved tools.
